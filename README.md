## 🚀 Key Features

1. **Dual Language Support (English & Natural Hindi):**
   - Instant language toggle in header and footer.
   - Medically authentic, natural Devanagari Hindi phrasing (not robotic translation).
   - Language preference saved in `localStorage`.

2. **Physiotherapist Branding & Verified Media:**
   - Real photograph of Dr. Archit Joshi (`images/archit_doct_phot.jpeg`) integrated into the Hero and Doctor Profile sections.
   - Highlights specialized qualification: **BPT, MPT (Neuro)**.

3. **Specialized Clinical Sections:**
   - **Orthopedic Physiotherapy:** Knee pain, back pain, neck/cervical pain, shoulder/frozen shoulder, heel pain, joint pain, slipped disc, sciatica, muscle strain, sports injury, post-surgery rehabilitation.
   - **Neuro Physiotherapy & Rehabilitation:** Stroke rehabilitation, paralysis mobility training, balance & coordination, task-oriented functional training.
   - **Other Services:** Pediatric physiotherapy, Home physiotherapy enquiry.

4. **Conversion & Appointment System:**
   - Interactive booking form with live 10-digit validation.
   - Automatic pre-fill of treatment concern from condition cards.
   - Direct 1-click **Book via WhatsApp** pre-formatted message generator (`https://wa.me/917690913118`).
   - Success modal with copy and WhatsApp dispatch options.

5. **Interactive Media & Lightbox:**
   - Category filtering (All, Doctor, Clinic, Treatments, Rehabilitation, Videos).
   - Responsive full-screen lightbox modal for image enlargement.
   - Video demonstration placeholders ready for future uploads.

6. **Local Alwar SEO & Schema.org:**
   - `MedicalBusiness` / `PhysiotherapyClinic` JSON-LD Structured Data with Alwar GeoCoordinates (27.5530, 76.6346), NAP data, and opening hours.
   - Semantic headings and targeted keywords (`Physiotherapist in Alwar`, `Orthopedic physiotherapy Alwar`, `Neuro physiotherapy Alwar`).

7. **Mobile-First UX & Accessibility:**
   - Sticky header and slide-in mobile navigation drawer.
   - Sticky bottom action bar on mobile devices (Call Now, Book Slot).
   - Floating WhatsApp button with animated pulse.
   - High contrast colors, accessible ARIA labels, and reduced motion support.

## 📸 Adding Future Content (Photos, Videos, Reviews)

- **Doctor & Clinic Photos:** Add new images to the `images/` directory and reference them in `index.html` under the `#gallery` section.
- **Videos:** Embed YouTube or MP4 video URLs into the `#video` cards or gallery video tabs.
- **Patient Reviews:** Add real patient reviews to the `#testimonials` section in `index.html` and corresponding entries in `js/translations.js`.
