About Our Centre

About Revital Ortho & Neuro Physiotherapy Centre

Revital Ortho & Neuro Physiotherapy Centre is a dedicated physiotherapy and rehabilitation clinic in Alwar, Rajasthan, established to help individuals overcome pain, recover from injuries, and regain functional independence.

Whether you are experiencing chronic joint pain, recovering from surgery, or managing neurological challenges such as stroke or paralysis, our centre provides compassionate, patient-centered care in a clean and well-equipped clinical environment.

We believe in a structured rehabilitation approach that combines targeted exercises, manual therapy techniques, patient education, and progressive functional training to support safe and sustained recovery.

Specialized Neurology Care

Neuro Physiotherapy & Rehabilitation

Specialized, compassionate rehabilitation focused on restoring movement, balance, coordination, and functional independence.

Neurological conditions require a methodical, patient, and scientifically grounded rehabilitation approach. At Revital, Dr. Archit Joshi (MPT Neuro) utilizes neuro-developmental and task-oriented techniques to help patients relearn movement patterns and maximize their physical capabilities.

Paralysis Rehabilitation

Structured exercise programs and assistive training aimed at facilitating muscle re-education, joint range of motion, and functional daily activity support.

Stroke Rehabilitation

Post-stroke mobility training, gait retraining, upper-limb functional practice, and neuro-motor coordination support.

Mobility & Functional Training

Targeted therapies to assist patients in transitioning from bed mobility to sitting, standing, and walking safely.

Balance & Coordination Support

Specialized balance training to reduce fall risks, enhance postural stability, and rebuild movement confidence.
